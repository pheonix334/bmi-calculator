How it works

  1. Choose your units. Toggle between metric (kg/cm) or imperial (lb/in).
  2. Enter your height and weight. These are the only two numbers BMI needs.
  3. Get your result instantly. Your BMI is calculated as weight ÷ height², then matched to a category (underweight, normal, overweight, obese) and shown on the color-coded scale.
  4. See your healthy range. The result also shows the weight range considered "normal BMI" for your specific height.

BMI is a quick screening number based on height and weight alone — it doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. Use it as a starting point, not a full picture of your health.
